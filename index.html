Our Philosophy

Designed for the way you live

PL Mens Wear started with a simple observation: most men dress for one occasion at a time — a shirt for work, another for the weekend, a third for the airport. We build clothing that moves effortlessly between those moments without missing a beat.

Every piece is wear-tested for months in real cities, real commutes and real Indian summers before it earns a permanent place in our line.
