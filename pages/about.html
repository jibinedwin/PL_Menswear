Designed for the way you live

Premium everyday menswear, designed in India — for work, weekend, travel and everything between.

The beginning

One wardrobe, many lives

PL Mens Wear started with a simple observation: most men dress for one occasion at a time — a shirt for work, another for the weekend, a third for the airport. We build clothing that moves between those moments without changing.

Every piece is wear-tested for months in real cities, real commutes and real summers before it earns a place in the line.

The method

Fewer, better pieces

We design a tight, deliberate range: shirts that survive nine-hour days, trousers with hidden comfort, knits with the hand-feel of far pricier yarns. When a fabric or fit is not right, it does not ship.
